LIMITATIONS / READ BEFORE USE

Scope of the records.

The sources included do not cover every country or language. A record count is not disease burden. Absence of a record does not show that no event occurred.

View current data statusRead the Markdown source

COVERAGE

Coverage

The choice of sources leaves out some countries, languages, institutions, and local channels.

TIMELINESS

Time

Check the data-as-of date in the manifest before use. Historical records without a reliable first-seen time are left empty.

EXTRACTION

Extraction

Dates, diseases, counts, places, summaries, and deduplication can be wrong. The quality score measures how complete the fields are.

GEOGRAPHY

Geography

Coordinates may be country or administrative centroids and are used to show distribution.

RIGHTS

Terms of use

The source may be read. Redistribution is not permitted. Third-party sources follow their own terms.

USE

Use

Risk scores order events. These data are not used for diagnosis or treatment.
