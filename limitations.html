LIMITATIONS / READ BEFORE USE

There is no complete global truth here.

EPIC is an incomplete, fallible public-source monitoring preview. Record counts are not disease burden, and missing records do not prove that no event occurred.

View current data statusRead the Markdown source

COVERAGE

Coverage is incomplete

Source selection misses countries, languages, institutions, and local channels, and may reflect geographic and media bias.

TIMELINESS

Snapshots can go stale

Always inspect the manifest. Historical records without a reliable first_seen_at return null rather than an invented timestamp.

EXTRACTION

Automated extraction can be wrong

Dates, diseases, counts, places, summaries, and deduplication can all be wrong. Quality scores reflect completeness and extraction confidence—not truth.

GEOGRAPHY

Map points are not field coordinates

Coordinates may be country or administrative centroids. They are for overview only and must not guide field action.

RIGHTS

Public does not mean reusable

This repository is not open source, and third-party sources retain their own terms. Read the license and source-rights notes before reuse.

DECISIONS

Not a substitute for professional judgment

EpiETL scores are not official risk levels. EPIC does not provide diagnosis, treatment, clinical, or emergency-response advice.
