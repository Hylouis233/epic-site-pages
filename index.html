06 METHOD & LIMITS

Make uncertainty part of the product.

EPIC is research infrastructure for open-source monitoring—not a validated official warning system. Every user should be able to see where data came from, how old it is, and which checks it passed.

INGEST

Separate freshness clocks

Build time, data-as-of time, and last successful ingest are tracked separately. A zero-record upstream response keeps the last-known-good snapshot without advancing data time.

NORMALIZE

Normalize event fields

Dates are separated into event, reporting-period, and publication semantics; disease aliases are canonicalized; scale is split into cases, deaths, and hospitalizations.

VALIDATE

Apply quality gates

Future dates, sudden record drops, source URLs, coordinates, duplicate signals, and schema validity are checked before publication.

LIMIT

State the boundaries

Coverage is incomplete, extraction can be wrong, geocoding may stop at country level, and risk ranking has not been validated for official public-health use.
