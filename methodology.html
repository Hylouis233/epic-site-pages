METHODOLOGY / SCHEMA V1

Write uncertainty into the data contract.

EPIC normalizes public-source signals into event-level summaries while keeping time, provenance, revision, and quality status traceable. It is research monitoring infrastructure—not an official public-health alert.

01 / TIME

Separate time semantics

Event start, event end, reporting period, publication, first observation, data-as-of, and build time are modeled separately. Future dates never enter sorting or data_as_of.

02 / ENTITY

Normalize diseases and events

Disease aliases map to stable disease_id values. Multi-disease bulletins retain an aggregate marker, while event, signal, and cluster identities remain distinct.

03 / SCALE

Keep quantities distinct

Natural-language scale is retained as scale_raw and, where possible, separated into cases, deaths, and hospitalizations. An event record is not a patient record or a measure of global burden.

04 / PROVENANCE

Preserve provenance

Public records retain a stable source URL, organization, type, rights note, and content hash. Full third-party source text is not republished.

05 / QUALITY

Gate before publishing

Builds check future dates, sudden record drops, source URLs, coordinates, duplicate signals, and schema validity. Upstream anomalies retain the last successful snapshot and are explicitly marked degraded.

06 / REPRODUCE

Make status reproducible

The manifest separates build_generated_at, data_as_of, and last_successful_ingest_at, and publishes SHA-256 checksums for every versioned artifact.
